8BALLTV SUBMISSION PREVIEW

About

This is a tool that makes 8 Ball TV submissions from Google Sheets look nicer.

You might have noticed that the link in your browser bar is a bazillion characters long. That’s because this site works by jamming all the Google Sheets text related to a submission into the url after a #, which makes it locally readable by this site’s javascript.

You cannot share links from this site with any ease. It is only intended as a tool to visually look at submissions from the Google Sheet easier.

Nothing on this page is published online. Everything is private. Everything is being served from your computer locally.

Enjoy!
